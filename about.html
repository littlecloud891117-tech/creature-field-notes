About the serial

Small choices leave large tracks.

Creature Field Notes is an independent serial about original creatures and the people who learn to care for them.

What to expect

Each episode works as a complete field story. The wider world grows through recurring places, consequences, and relationships.

The serial uses no characters, names, worlds, or visual designs from an existing franchise.
