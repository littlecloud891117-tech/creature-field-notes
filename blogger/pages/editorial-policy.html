Editorial Policy

Tools can support drafting and production. A person makes material creative decisions before each episode becomes public.

Each episode receives a complete Traditional Chinese review copy. The reviewer changes or decides a character motive, relationship, dialogue, event result, or creature trait.

The English episode changes to match those decisions. The reviewer checks the final English text before publication.

Monetization

Advertisements remain disabled until Google approves the site. Affiliate links, if added later, appear only on separate curated pages with a clear disclosure.
